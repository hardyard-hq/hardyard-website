Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your data.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. NO-BS SUMMARY

We collect some of your data so the app actually works. We don't sell your data to random ad networks. We secure it, use it for its intended purpose, and that's it.

2. WHAT WE COLLECT AND WHY

  • Location Data (GPS): We need your live location to show you relevant marketplace listings in The Yard (like nearby materials or muck away jobs). If you trigger the SOS Safety feature, your location is sent directly to your emergency contacts.
  • Camera Access: We require camera permissions for The Shield (our tool scanner) to scan QR codes, barcodes, and read serial numbers using OCR. We also use the camera for snapping site photos for the Vault and Yard listings.
  • Contact Information: We store the phone numbers of your emergency contacts and your own number if you provide it for the Yard marketplace.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

  • To provide core app functionality (Marketplace, Safety SOS, Tool Scanning, Vault documentation).
  • To sync your data to our secure Supabase backend, ensuring your records are available if you switch devices.

4. DATA SHARING

We don't sell your info. We may share necessary data with authorities ONLY if legally required (e.g., investigating stolen tools via The Shield).

When you post in The Yard marketplace, other users will see your general location and contact info to arrange trades. Don't post what you don't want seen.

5. SECURITY

We use industry-standard security (Supabase) to protect your stuff. But nothing on the internet is 100% secure. Use common sense.

6. YOUR RIGHTS

You own your data. You have the right to:

  • Access all data we hold about you.
  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time.
  • Request a copy of your data by contacting hello@hardyardhq.com.
  • All requests will be handled within 30 days.

7. DATA RETENTION

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Deleted items are removed within 30 days. If you request full account deletion, all data is permanently removed within 30 days.

8. DATA CONTROLLER

HardYard is the data controller responsible for your personal data.
HardYard is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). Registration number: ZC169893.
Contact: hello@hardyardhq.com

9. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING (UK GDPR)

HardYard processes your personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. Our lawful basis for processing is performance of a contract — we process your data to provide the services you have signed up for. Where we process location data for the SOS safety feature, we do so on the basis of your explicit consent, which you may withdraw at any time via your device settings.

10. ANALYTICS AND SCAN LOGGING

  • Analytics: We use PostHog (EU servers) to see which features are used so we can improve the app. This activity is linked to your account.
  • Street Check logging: When you search a serial number in The Shield, we log the serial number, the time, your approximate location, and which account made the search. If the serial matches a tool registered by another user, that owner is told it was checked, and roughly where, but never who checked it. This log may be disclosed to law enforcement on lawful request.
